A one-sentence pitch for a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you
realise it would actually sell — entered in the make.fun bounty
[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a).

## The pitch

> Rent-a-Grandma: lonely professionals pay $49 a month for a grandmother who
> cooks Sunday dinner, scolds their life choices, and knits them a sweater -
> cheaper than therapy, warmer than any app.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes that single sentence —
at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`.
